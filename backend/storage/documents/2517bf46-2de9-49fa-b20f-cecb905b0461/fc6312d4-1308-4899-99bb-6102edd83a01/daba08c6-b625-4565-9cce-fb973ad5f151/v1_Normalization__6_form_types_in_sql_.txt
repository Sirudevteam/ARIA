✅ 1. First Normal Form (1NF)
Rule: No repeating groups or arrays. Each column must contain atomic values.

🚫 Unnormalized Table:
StudentID	Name	Courses
1	Alice	Math, English
2	Bob	Science, Math

✅ 1NF Table:
StudentID	Name	Course
1	Alice	Math
1	Alice	English
2	Bob	Science
2	Bob	Math

✅ 2. Second Normal Form (2NF)
Rule: Must be in 1NF and no partial dependency (non-key attribute depends on whole primary key).

🚫 1NF Table with Partial Dependency:
StudentID	CourseID	StudentName	CourseName
1	C101	Alice	Math

StudentName depends only on StudentID

✅ 2NF (Split Tables):
Students Table

StudentID	StudentName
1	Alice

Courses Table

CourseID	CourseName
C101	Math

Enrollments Table

StudentID	CourseID
1	C101

✅ 3. Third Normal Form (3NF)
Rule: Must be in 2NF and no transitive dependency (non-key depends on another non-key).

🚫 2NF Table:
EmployeeID	Name	DeptID	DeptName
101	John	10	HR

DeptName depends on DeptID, which is not the primary key.

✅ 3NF (Split):
Departments Table

DeptID	DeptName
10	HR

Employees Table

EmployeeID	Name	DeptID
101	John	10

✅ 4. Boyce-Codd Normal Form (BCNF)
Rule: Stronger version of 3NF — every determinant must be a candidate key.

🚫 Problem Table:
Course	Instructor	Room
Math	Alice	R1

Course → Instructor

Instructor → Room
But Instructor is not a candidate key.

✅ BCNF Solution:
Split into:
Course_Instructor

Course	Instructor
Math	Alice

Instructor_Room

Instructor	Room
Alice	R1

✅ 5. Fourth Normal Form (4NF)
Rule: Must be in BCNF and no multi-valued dependencies.

🚫 Table with Multi-Valued Dependencies:
Student	Course	Hobby
Alice	Math	Chess
Alice	Math	Painting

Independent sets of data combined.

✅ 4NF Tables:
Student_Course

Student	Course
Alice	Math

Student_Hobby

Student	Hobby
Alice	Chess
Alice	Painting

✅ 6. Fifth Normal Form (5NF)
Rule: Deals with join dependency — data should not be reconstructible from multiple joins.

🚫 3-way relationship Table:
Supplier	Product	Location
S1	P1	L1

Split into:
Supplier_Product

Supplier	Product

Product_Location

Product	Location

Supplier_Location

Supplier	Location

Reconstruction must give original table using natural joins.
